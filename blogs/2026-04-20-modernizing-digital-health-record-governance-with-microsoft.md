The digital transformation of healthcare continues to accelerate. Clinicians expect near-instant access to Electronic Health Records (EHRs), clinical workflows increasingly span cloud and on-premises systems, and regulatory pressures around identity, access, and auditability have never been higher. For healthcare security and IT leaders, one challenge consistently rises to the top: ensuring the right clinicians have the right access to EHR systems—no more, no less—throughout their lifecycle.
