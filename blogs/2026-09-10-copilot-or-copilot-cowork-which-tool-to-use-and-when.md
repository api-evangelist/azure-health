Microsoft 365 Copilot vs Copilot Cowork: Choosing the Right AI for Work As organizations expand their use of AI to improve productivity and automate business processes, understanding which tool to use—and when—is critical. Microsoft offers two complementary capabilities: Microsoft 365 Copilot and Copilot Cowork. While they both leverage AI to support work, they are designed for fundamentally different types of tasks.
