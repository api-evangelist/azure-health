At HIMSS 2026, we showed that the most useful place to apply clinical intelligence is often not in a separate app or side window, but at the cursor, in the field where the clinician is already working. Our last post on this topic described the problem Dragon Copilot's focus capability is designed to solve: clinicians becoming data couriers, moving between windows, copying information from one system into another, and stitching together a day's work across multiple applications. That post looked ahead to what was coming.
