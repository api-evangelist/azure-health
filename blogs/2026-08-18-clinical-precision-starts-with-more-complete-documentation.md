This blog is authored by Josh Waldo, Product Manager Every patient has a story, and that story deserves to be accurately reflected in the clinical documentation. Clinicians work hard to assess, diagnose, and care for patients, often while balancing complex cases, competing priorities, and limited time. While the diagnosis itself may be clear, the documentation does not always capture every detail that helps tell the full patient story.
