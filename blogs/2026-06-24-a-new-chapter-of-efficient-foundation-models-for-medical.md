Authors : Ivan Tarapov, Naiteek Sangani, Mu Wei, Noel Codella, Mert Oez and Naveen Valluri, Microsoft Healthcare and Life Sciences At HLTH 2024, we introduced three open-source healthcare AI foundation models on Microsoft Foundry : MedImageInsight (MI2) , CxrReportGen (CXRRG) , and MedImageParse (MIP) . They quickly became the most popular healthcare industry models in the Foundry catalog, with consistent, growing usage by researchers, ISVs, and clinical teams over more than 15 months. Customer momentum has been real: SECTRA has explored integrating MedImageInsight for real-time exam parameter determination.
