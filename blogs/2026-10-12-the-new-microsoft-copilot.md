The way we work with AI is evolving quickly. Microsoft recently introduced the new Microsoft Copilot experience, bringing together new ways to ask, delegate, build and automate work while giving organizations additional controls for managing AI usage and spend. In our recent webinar, “New Microsoft Copilot App: Which Mode to Use, What It Costs, and Who Gets Access,” we took a closer look at what these changes mean for organizations and the people using Copilot every day.
