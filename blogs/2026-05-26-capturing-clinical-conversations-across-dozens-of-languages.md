Language can shape trust, understanding, and the patient experience Patient care doesn’t happen in just one language. To better reflect the diversity of patients and communities physicians serve, Dragon Copilot now supports dozens of languages, enabling clinicians to capture clinical conversations naturally without changing how they document care. This expanded capability helps physicians engage more confidently with diverse patient populations, transition smoothly from one appointment to the next, and spend more time focused on care rather than documentation.
